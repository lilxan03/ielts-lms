Phần 1: Mini Reading (5 câu)

Urban vertical farms use controlled indoor lighting and hydroponic systems to grow leafy greens year-round. Because water is recirculated in a closed loop, these farms consume up to 90% less water than conventional open-field agriculture. However, high electricity costs for LED lighting remain a major barrier for growing staple crops such as wheat or rice indoors.
